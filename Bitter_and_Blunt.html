About

Bitter and Blunt is a song released by YonKaGor on November 29th, 2019. It is the 4th track on the Paper Alibis EP. The main character featured in the video is Honest Lie, accompanied by Vince, Jazz, and March, MelloRange's OC's, and various cameos from YonKaGor's patreon supporters as background characters.

Meaning

Bitter and Blunt is about someone coping and leaving a toxic or abusive relationship. The singer doubts themselves and their parting at first, though eventually finds the self confidence to leave the situation and be around those who care about them.

Trivia

  • Bitter and Blunt and Artificial Abandonment are connected, and tell a story. Though, they were not originally written to be connected.[1]
  • BaB was originally going to be scrapped due to its personal nature, until YonKaGor decided otherwise.[2]
  • Multiple cameo appearances can be seen in the music video, consisting of patreon supporters, MelloRange's characters, and close friends of YonKaGor.
    • All cameos featured in the music video include: Elmira, Fiakaiera, GinjaNinjaOWO, Huntereon, Icecolo, MelloRange, Milkyy, Riko Sakari, sleepiitears, Soll0127, ZameerDraws and Zenonyte.[3]
    • BaB and Silly Plans are the only two music videos to feature character Hickory.
    • BnB was accidentally deleted from YouTube by Yon themselves, but was later restored.[4]
