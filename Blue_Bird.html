About

Blue Bird is a song released by Ivycomb and YonKaGor on May 19th, 2023. The characters featured in the video are Moka and Tetra.

Meaning

Blue Bird is the about the addictiveness of social media (In this case, Twitter) and it's impact on relationships. The main singer is neglecting their responsibilities and friendship with Tetra because of their preoccupation with online spaces. In this way, it is comparable to that of You're Just Like Pop Music.

Trivia

  • Blue Bird is the first time YonKaGor and Ivycomb have collaborated, with the econd being INFAMOUS.
  • The title "Blue Bird" is a reference to the old twitter logo.
