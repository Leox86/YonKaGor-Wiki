About

Butcher Vanity is a short instrumental cover of FLAVOUR FOLEY's "BUTCHER VANITY" uploaded by YonKaGor on June 3rd, 2024.

Trivia

  • The laughing sound effects YonKaGor used was their own laugh as they were recording.[1]
  • The description states “Yonkavore”, as a portmanteau on YonKaGor and vore.
  • The art was originally planned to have André Moraph instead of the fish, but was changed due to making the YonKaVore joke not land, instead making Yon "look like a freak"[2]
