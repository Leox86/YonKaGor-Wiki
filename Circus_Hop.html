About

Circus Hop is a song released by YonKaGor on July 19th, 2020. The song is notable for its happy sounding instrumentals juxtaposed with its dark and depressing lyrics. The main character featured in the video is Clownfish, as well as Vince, Jazz, and March as background characters.

Meaning

Circus Hop is about the neglect of mental health in the Eastern schooling system.[1] Circus Hop depicts a crazed, fanatic character being bullied and eventually being pushed to commit suicide. The pre-chorus is from the perspective of people who actually care, while the line "The acrobat who's waiting at the top / Should do a circus hop" is a quote from the bullies.[2][3]
