About

Cherries and Wine is a cancelled WIP song posted to Twitter by YonKaGor on July 6th, 2020.

Lyrics

I saw a creature attempting to fly Gasping through echoes, it flew from it's cries Lost in a forest of colorless trees In a rainfall of moral deceit Losing it's promising hue Stained with the pigments of wisdom As it neglected its youth So, it painted branches with cherries and wine They'll taste sweeter than lies "What a miserable mockery" Still, I'm wavin' goodbye, wavin' goodbye


Trivia

  • Blue Bird was one of the four cancelled songs posted publicly on Twitter by Yon, with the other three being I stay the same, Tell Me, and a remix of Another Mistake.
  • Yon has expressed repeated interest in renewing CaW.[1][2]
